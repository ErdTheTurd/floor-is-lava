Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

Lava Floor AR is an iOS AR game published by Edward Dunn (ERD). This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with information on your device.

We do not collect data

Lava Floor AR does not collect personal data. The app does not create an account, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send information to Edward Dunn or to any third party.

Camera and LiDAR (on-device only)

Solo AR and multiplayer AR use the camera (and LiDAR mesh reconstruction on supported devices) so the app can place lava and safe spots in your room. That processing happens on your device. Lava Floor AR does not record video, does not save room scans to iCloud, and does not upload camera or mesh data.

Local network (on-device / room only)

Multiplayer uses Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity / local-network APIs so nearby iPhones and iPads can find each other and sync a match. Traffic stays on the local network. The game does not require the internet, and session data is not uploaded to a server.

What stays on your device

Settings and in-session game state live on your iPhone or iPad. If you delete the app, that on-device data is removed with it.

Offline by design

After install, Lava Floor AR works without a network connection. The Practice Room never needs the camera. Multiplayer needs only a local network between the devices in the room.

No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. There are no ads and no third-party measurement tools in the app.

Kids and families

The game is meant for shared play at home. Because we do not collect data or require accounts, there is no profile for a child to create.
