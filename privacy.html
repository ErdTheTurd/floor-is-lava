Privacy

Updated November 2026

Hi. I am Edward Dunn (ERD), and I made Lava Floor AR. Parents ask about privacy a lot, so here is the straight answer.

I don't collect data

No accounts. No analytics kit. No ads. The app does not send info to me or to anyone else.

Camera and LiDAR

When you play in AR, the camera (and LiDAR on phones and iPads that have it) helps place the lava and find couches, cushions, and tables. That work happens on your device. The app does not record video, does not save room scans to iCloud, and does not upload any of that.

Local network

Multiplayer uses Apple’s local network tools so nearby devices can find each other. Traffic stays in the room. You do not need the internet, and match data does not go to a server.

Stuff on your phone

Settings and the current game stay on the device. Delete the app and that goes with it.

Offline

After install, Solo AR, Pass & Play, and the Practice Room work with no network. The Practice Room never needs the camera. Multiplayer only needs the local network between the devices you are using.

Kids

This is meant for playing at home with friends and family. Because there are no accounts and no data collection, there is no kid profile to set up.
