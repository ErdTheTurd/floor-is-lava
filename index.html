Lava Floor AR

Your living room floor turns into lava.

Jump from the couch to the ottoman before it eats your socks. LiDAR phones and iPads spot the raised furniture. Friends can pile on over the same Wi-Fi. No internet. No accounts. App Store screenshots are still in progress, so this page stays short for now.

How you play

On a LiDAR iPhone or iPad, point at the room. Couches, cushions, and tables turn into safe spots with teal edges. The lava keeps rising during the round, so you keep moving.

Friends play on the same Wi-Fi. One person hosts, everyone else joins. Nothing needs the internet, and nobody makes an account.

Do not want to scan yet? Open the Practice Room. It is a tiny living room already set up, with the same rising lava and no camera.

The app does not collect data. Camera and LiDAR stay on your device. More on the privacy page. Stuck? Support.
