Lava Floor AR

Your room. Rising lava. Safe spots only.

Point the camera at your living room and the floor turns into molten rock. Couches, cushions, and tables become the pads you jump between. I built this so you can race friends without leaving the house — and without needing the internet.

LiDAR safe spots

Your couch is the island.

On LiDAR iPhones and iPads, the app scans the room and looks for raised surfaces — furniture, cushions, pillows, chairs, beds, tables. Those become glowing teal pads. The floor is the lava. Stay up.

No LiDAR? You can still play Solo AR with plane detection and tap-to-place pads. Or skip scanning and open the Practice Room.

Molten floor

It looks hot because it should.

Dark cooling plates, bright crack veins, drifting hot pools, bubbles, sparks, and a low rumble that gets louder as the lava climbs. Your phone also buzzes when you get too close — so you feel the rise, not just watch a meter.

Same lava in AR and in the Practice Room. I wanted the magma to feel like one thing, not two different games.

Same room, many devices

Race on your Wi‑Fi. No accounts.

Host a lobby on one iPhone or iPad. Friends join from theirs on the same local network. Countdown, timer, lava level, and scores stay in sync — all over Multipeer, with no internet and nothing uploaded.

One device? Use Pass & Play for 2–6 people taking turns. Fastest completed time wins.

Practice Room

Try the lava without scanning.

Open a built-in living room — couch, ottoman, cushions, coffee table — and run rounds with no camera. Great for learning the timer and the rise before you clear the coffee table in real life.

When you escape, the leaderboard shows who was fastest. Then hand the phone over, or start another round.

On your devices

Camera and LiDAR stay home.

Lava Floor AR does not create accounts and does not collect data. The camera, LiDAR mesh, and local-network link are used on-device so you can play. Nothing is sent to me or to a server.
