FAQ

Which devices have LiDAR?

Automatic safe spots need an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR. That covers recent Pro iPhones (12 Pro and later Pro models) and several iPad Pro models. Apple’s list changes, so check your device specs if you are unsure.

No LiDAR? You can still play Solo AR by tapping to place pads, or skip scanning and use the Practice Room.

How does multiplayer work?

Everyone needs an iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi (or a hotspot). One person taps Host. Others tap Join and allow Local Network. When people show up in the lobby, the host starts the round. Timer, lava level, and scores stay in sync. No internet. No accounts.

Only one device? Pass & Play lets 2 to 6 people take turns. Fastest time wins.

What is the Practice Room?

A built-in living room with a couch, ottoman, cushions, and a coffee table. Same rising lava and timer, but no camera. Good for learning, or for days when you cannot shove the real furniture around.

Does it need the internet?

No. After install, Solo AR, Pass & Play, and the Practice Room work offline. Multiplayer only needs the local network between the devices in the room.

Is any data collected?

No. No accounts, no analytics. Camera, LiDAR, and local-network traffic stay on your devices. See the privacy page.

Friends cannot find the lobby

Check that Local Network is allowed for Lava Floor AR in Settings, that everyone is on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, and that Bluetooth is on. Have the host leave and make a new lobby, then join again.
